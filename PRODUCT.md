# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

South African B2B decision-makers: procurement officers, CTOs, facilities managers, and corporate buyers. Ages 30–55. Experienced evaluators who have seen hundreds of generic corporate sites. They make high-stakes buying decisions and need to trust MPSM within 8 seconds or they move on. They are not browsing; they are vetting. Context: reviewing during a workday, often on desktop, under time pressure. Job: determine whether MPSM is credible, capable, and the right fit for their specific divisional need.

## Product Purpose

MPSM Services is a Level 1 BBBEE South African enterprise, entirely owned and managed by Black women, operating across four divisions: IT Consulting and Infrastructure, Water Purification and Beverage Supply, Procurement (PaaS), and Energy Solutions. The site's purpose is to establish trust, demonstrate expertise, and convert qualified interest into contact. Success: a decision-maker arrives on the home page and, within 8 seconds, feels they have found a serious, capable enterprise partner.

## Brand Personality

Warm authority. Senior partner energy — not cold corporate, not startup-casual. Three words: **grounded, capable, purposeful**. The brand is confident without posturing. It uses plain language and does not hide behind jargon. Warm but not informal. South African enterprise with global ambition.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages: gradient text, nested cards, purple palettes, glassmorphism, dark glows, bounce easing
- Dark-mode-with-gold-accents corporate sites
- AI-generated sites: gradient text, identical icon-tile card grids, repeated section label scaffolding on every heading
- Editorial-magazine aesthetic (Klim-influenced, display-serif-italic, mono track labels, ruled separators) — too European, not distinctly South African
- Any design that could belong to any country, any category, any brand

## Design Principles

1. **Trust before beauty.** Every decision asks: does this make a procurement officer trust us? Beauty that does not serve trust gets cut.
2. **Grounded in place.** The visual language draws from South African earth — ochre, clay, sage, warm stone — not European editorial conventions. This site should feel unmistakably South African.
3. **One voice, sustained.** Section kicker labels are used sparingly (3 times maximum across the entire site). Typography scale and spacing create hierarchy everywhere else.
4. **Restraint is earned.** Remove an element only if its removal makes the design stronger. Subtract for clarity, not for minimalism's sake.
5. **Division clarity.** Each of the 4 divisions feels distinct on its page — not a template with different icons. Art direction varies per division while the voice stays consistent.

## Accessibility and Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. All interactive elements have visible focus states. Reduced-motion users receive no GSAP entrance animations. Colour choices must achieve 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text. Tap targets minimum 44x44px on mobile. Design for variable connection quality: no heavy unoptimised assets.
